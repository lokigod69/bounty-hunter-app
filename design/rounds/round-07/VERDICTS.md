# 2026-09-07 — Distinct rims, shared numerals retained

Michael likes the numeral artwork but says the three coin bases look too similar, especially their repeated diagonal hooks. He authorizes optional refinement while prioritizing security/release.

Direct refinement within locked A/B/C: Starlight remains; Forged gets a hammered, irregular minted rim; Astral gets an octagonal optical-glass/platinum rim. The existing 0–9 raster alphabet is unchanged and sits on each solid face. No new skin, currency, number alphabet or settings page.

Two edited candidates had baked checkerboards and were rejected. Built-in generation produced actual-alpha replacements. Export is mechanical trim, proportional 256px containment and WebP compression. Both bases total 55,862 bytes, replacing 50,528 bytes (+5,334 bytes).
