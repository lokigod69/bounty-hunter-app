Direction A — STARLIGHT GLASS. My strongest recommendation: jewel-quality transparent smoked crystal against deep navy space. The physical material is almost invisible in the middle, with a thin polished glass lip that catches mint-white light on the upper-left edge and tiny controlled caustic reflections in the corners. The hovered first card has an especially beautiful clear center with two stars visible through it, slightly refracted by the curved edge. Deep black/navy shell, restrained gold coin. The title MISSIONS is a bespoke frosted-silver Mandalore inscription with subtle bevel and a narrow mint rim, an artwork of icy silver without unreadable shine. The segmented active tab is a thin clear mint-tinted lens; inactive remains transparent. New mission is a small crafted transparent crystal button, faint sculpted glass ends, bright readable pale-mint lettering, visible stars inside. Material detail is premium and rich at the edges, exceptionally quiet behind text. Maintain the current simple rounded card silhouette; no oversized glow, no neon blanket. An original collectible-object finish applied to a useful readable interface.

Retain mint in active control edge light; brass is a controlled secondary finish, not an overall brown palette. Use very fine precision engraving, slight real metal irregularity, a few tiny inset gem catches and layered corner clasps, never a thick medieval game border. The centered card interior remains clear glass and clean readable navy/white text, with the stars underneath. The title MISSIONS uses the Mandalore cut-out geometric display alphabet, forged pale platinum faces with worn dark sides and minute brass detail, beautiful as a physical title asset. The first card hover lights its upper-left metal catch and subtly brightens its glass. Rich craft and convincing fine texture, controlled so the original layout remains airy. Do not add a decorative hero, character, weapon, stickers, fake HUD labels or content imagery.

Direction C — ASTRAL LENS. An unusual interface made from wafer-thin transparent optical material, subtly asymmetric clipped corners and floating glass rails with a very small spectral split at their edges. Rich clear dark glass refracts a few stars at the border; keep the overall navy/mint/gold brand palette with restrained violet/cyan interference only where edges catch light. The title MISSIONS is Mandalore geometric lettering cut from silver optical glass, gently prismatic beveled edges and solid readable silver faces. Give the heading a narrow hand-etched orbital line fragment as a material detail, not a new illustration. The selected For you tab and New mission button are precision optical lenses with different silhouettes from the cards, no solid colored backgrounds. The hovered first card looks like a glass pane focusing starlight into a controlled corner glint. Content is stable and readable, never a holographic text ghost. Card frames should feel strange and designed beyond stock web controls, but the layout, task content, spacing and proportions remain as in the supplied screen. Avoid a busy videogame dashboard, huge rainbow gradients, blue neon outlines, decorative data, or opaque frosted panels.
