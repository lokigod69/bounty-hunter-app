## Transparency edits

Initial RGB checkerboard exports were rejected. Controls: “Replace the checkerboard behind this frame with solid pure white. Make both the center hole and the outside background plain white. Keep the frame unchanged. No checkerboard anywhere.” Then “Make the white background transparent, including the center hole. Keep the frame unchanged.”

Forged/Astral ring white-ground outputs received the same transparency edit. Starlight ring already had alpha. Production exports were checked for actual alpha and transparent center/corner pixels, then cropped/resized/compressed with Sharp. No bitmap text is used.
